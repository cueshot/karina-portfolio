The Photographer

Karina

Born and raised in New York, I came to wedding photography through a love of film and a deep fascination with human connection. After years spent shooting portraits and editorial work across the city, I found my calling on the wedding day — where emotion, light, and story all converge in a single afternoon. For over a decade I have been photographing weddings in New York, across Europe, and throughout the American countryside.

My approach is quiet and observant. I believe the most powerful photographs happen in the spaces between — the breath before the vows, the hand held tightly beneath the table, the way two people look at each other when they think no one is watching. Working in a documentary style drawn from fine-art influences, I create images that feel timeless rather than trendy, and wholly, unmistakably yours.
